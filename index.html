Commas and full stops pause, ? lifts the last word, ! starts high, ... trails off and CAPITALS shout. Add {purr}, {snort}, {sigh}, {hmm} or {laugh} anywhere, and wrap words in {whisper}…{/whisper} to whisper them. Ctrl+Enter speaks.

Writing tips

Start a line with L: for the left person or R: for the right one. A line with no tag goes to whoever did not speak last. ? lifts the last word, ! starts high, ... trails off and CAPITALS shout. Add {purr}, {snort}, {sigh}, {hmm} or {laugh} anywhere, and wrap words in {whisper}…{/whisper} to whisper them. Ctrl+Enter plays.
